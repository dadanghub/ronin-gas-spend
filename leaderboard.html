Spend gas

Heavy slots —
Calls logActivity(bytes32) or logActivityHeavy(bytes32,uint256) on the contract from your connected wallet. Every call costs real gas paid in RON — that's exactly what PoD scores under Gas Spend. No wallet connected yet? Clicking a button will prompt you to connect. First call auto-registers you. Heavy burn writes thousands of permanent on-chain records: at 2,000 slots ≈ 45M gas ≈ ~0.95 RON per call (≈76% of a block). Estimates update live with the current gas price.
